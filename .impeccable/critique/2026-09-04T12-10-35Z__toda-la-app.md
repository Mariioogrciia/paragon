## Overall Impression

The new work (price chart, discover rewrite) is genuinely good and on-brand — the concern isn't quality, it's that it landed without the safety net round 1 already flagged, and introduced one new instance of exactly that gap: `.animate-glint`'s `overflow: hidden` clips `StatTile`'s own hover shadow, silently killing hover feedback on 7 tiles — the same "overflow-hidden eats its own effect" trap HANDOFF.md already documents once (there with `filter: drop-shadow`), and a direct hit against your own stored rule that every clickable control needs a visible hover state. ## Priority Issues

**[P1] New regression: `.animate-glint`'s `overflow:hidden` clips `StatTile`'s own hover shadow**
- Why it matters: `StatTile.tsx` puts `animate-glint` and a hover box-shadow on the same element; a non-inset box-shadow is clipped by `overflow:hidden` on its own box in every major browser. Kills hover feedback on all `StatTile` instances (3× homepage stat row, 4× `TrophyHistory` streak tiles) — this is the exact pattern your own stored rule calls out (every clickable control needs a visible hover state), and it's new since round 1.
- Fix: move `overflow:hidden` to an inner wrapper that contains only the glint sweep; keep the hover shadow on the un-clipped outer box, matching how `GameCard.tsx` already does it correctly per HANDOFF.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Still open: no error boundaries, now covering a wider async surface**
- Why it matters: zero `error.tsx`/`global-error.tsx` in `src/app`, only one nested `loading.tsx`. The homepage now runs a 6-way `Promise.all` with no skeleton and no failure path — broader exposure than round 1 measured.
- Fix: `app/error.tsx` + `app/global-error.tsx` with plain-language Spanish recovery; root `loading.tsx` matching the new stat/chart layout.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Price chart is mouse-only; range toggle has no accessible selected state**
- Why it matters: the chart's only way to read a day's price/store is `onMouseMove` — no touch equivalent, so its entire interactive payoff is unreachable on mobile. The 7D/1M/3M/1A/Todo buttons convey the active range through color alone, no `aria-pressed`.
- Fix: add tap/click handling with nearest-point snapping; `aria-pressed` on range buttons.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P2] Still open, now confirmed harder: `/juego/[id]` has zero `<h1>` in the document**
- Why it matters: not a conditional gap — real DOM evidence shows no `<h1>` anywhere on `/juego/3278`, the app's flagship, most-shared page.
- Fix: visually-hidden `<h1>` with the game title next to the logo image.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P3] Homepage has no way to collapse or reorder its ~12 sections**
- Why it matters: `/u/[handle]` already has a working drag-to-reorder section system (`lib/profileSections.ts`) the homepage doesn't reuse — every visit pays the full scroll cost.
- Fix: extend the existing section-order mechanism to the homepage, or default low-signal sections (Juegos parados, Jugado recientemente) to collapsed.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

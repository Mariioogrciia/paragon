Paragon brings your PlayStation trophies and your Steam and Xbox achievements together in one place. Just enter your public ID: no passwords or tokens from any platform.

🏆 YOUR PROGRESS, CLEARLY
• Your whole library with the progress of every game, across the three platforms.
• A diary for every platinum: when you earned each trophy, your hardest wall and your biggest feat.
• Stats for your week, your year and all time, your hunting style and your favourite hour.
• Focus mode: the next trophy to go for and what's left for the platinum.

👥 WITH YOUR FRIENDS
• Monthly league, private leagues and clans: compete with anyone you like.
• Compare your trophies with a friend's.
• Sessions to earn online trophies as a group.
• Share your platinums with a card ready for Instagram or TikTok.

🔔 DON'T MISS A THING
• Notifications when you earn a trophy, when a friend overtakes you in the league or when a game you want drops in price.
• A widget with your pinned game.

Paragon only reads your public data. On PlayStation, your trophy profile must be public; on Steam, your profile and "Game details". The app tells you exactly what's missing.

Available in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Portuguese.
